## 1. Instructions and roles

The School determines the purposes and legal basis for its educational processing. The Service processes school-provided personal data only to provide the requested classroom-game, live-quiz, account-management, AI-generation, and shared-storage features, on the School's documented instructions and as required by applicable law.

## 2. Processing details

- Duration: while the School account or relevant feature is active, subject to the deletion periods below.
- People concerned: teachers, school administrators, invited staff, students, and support contacts.
- Data: names or classroom labels, email addresses for adult accounts and invitations, account roles, quiz-game content, uploaded materials, live-quiz answers/scores/timing, limited teacher activity records, support communications, and necessary technical/security records.
- Purposes: authentication, classroom content creation and storage, live quizzes, sharing, school administration, support, security, quota management, and service operation.
- Sensitive data: the Service is not intended for unnecessary special-category or highly sensitive student data. The School must not submit it unless separately agreed and lawfully authorised.

## 3. Processor duties

The Service will:

- process personal data only on documented instructions from the School;
- ensure authorised personnel are bound by confidentiality;
- maintain appropriate technical and organisational security measures;
- assist the School, taking account of the nature of the processing, with data-subject requests, security incidents, impact assessments, and regulator consultations;
- notify the School without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal-data breach affecting School data;
- provide information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance and permit proportionate audits under agreed confidentiality and scheduling terms;
- delete or return School personal data when the service ends, unless applicable law requires retention; and
- not use School personal data for advertising or to train general-purpose AI models for the Service's or a provider's benefit.

## 4. Security measures

Measures include encrypted HTTPS transport, managed authentication, access rules that restrict teacher and school records, private object storage with time-limited links, server-side API credentials, limited administrative access, automated live-quiz deletion, account erasure, dependency and access reviews, backups governed by provider retention, and incident-response procedures.

## 5. Subprocessors

The School gives general written authorisation for the subprocessors published in the Service's current subprocessor list. The Service will provide reasonable advance notice of a material new subprocessor so the School can raise a reasoned data-protection objection.

The Service remains responsible for ensuring subprocessors are bound by data-protection obligations appropriate to their role.

## 6. International transfers

Personal data will be transferred outside the EEA only where an applicable adequacy decision or another lawful safeguard applies. Where required, the parties will rely on the relevant European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses and any necessary supplementary measures.

## 7. Retention

- Live-quiz participant labels, answers, scores, timings, and their parent sessions: removed after reaching 24 hours old.
- Expired school-invitation records: removed after a further 30-day administration/security window.
- AI usage and security records: no more than 12 months.
- Account content and shared-school content: for the active service period, then deleted or returned as agreed.
- Provider backups and security logs: according to the verified provider schedule recorded in **[link or schedule attachment]**.
